# CCC v10 Regenerative Revenue Flywheel

State: VERIFY
Authority: CCC Canon -> Ledger factual state -> Revenue Assurance -> Freedom Architect allocation -> human approval

## Purpose

CCC is designed to create multiple lawful revenue pathways that reinforce one another instead of depending on a single well. The objective is not to claim infinite or guaranteed returns. The objective is to maintain a continuously replenished pipeline of evidence-backed opportunities, kill weak branches early, protect liquidity, retain customers through measurable outcomes, and route verified surplus into the next productive constraint.

## Closed Loop

KNOWLEDGE -> CAPABILITY -> EVIDENCE -> PRODUCT/SERVICE -> PRODUCT FOUNDRY -> MARKET/DEMAND -> CONTRACT/SALE -> DELIVERY -> CUSTOMER SUCCESS -> INVOICE -> COLLECTION -> REVENUE ASSURANCE -> RECONCILED SURPLUS -> FREEDOM ARCHITECT CAPITAL ALLOCATION -> PRODUCTIVE CAPACITY -> CUSTOMER LEARNING -> NEXT ALPHA

Every cycle must return at least one productive output: realized revenue, recurring-revenue potential, reusable IP, verified skill, evidence, customer demand, customer learning, compliance readiness, operational efficiency, productive asset value, or a better next decision. If a branch produces none, Mission Control recommends HOLD -> MODIFY -> KILL.

## Recurring Customer Cycle

Sale-ready work must enter a second loop designed to produce retention and feedback rather than treating the first transaction as the finish line:

SALE_READY -> ACQUIRE -> ONBOARD -> DELIVER -> MEASURE_OUTCOME -> SUPPORT -> RENEW/EXPAND -> REFER/ADVOCATE -> FEEDBACK_TO_FOUNDRY -> IMPROVE_OFFER

A payment alone does not establish a healthy recurring stream. CCC must record the promised outcome, measured outcome, delivery state, SLA/SLO state where applicable, support state, renewal/expansion signal, customer feedback, and provenance. Customer learning returns to Product Foundry and the next Alpha.

## Revenue Stream Registry

The machine-readable registry is `config/revenue-stream-registry.yaml`.

Initial controlled lanes include:

- remote cybersecurity employment
- cybersecurity / SOC contract services
- Living Intelligence custom implementations
- Living Intelligence recurring analytics / SaaS
- managed security and analytics
- Product Foundry / Digital Twin validation
- cybersecurity and operating-intelligence training
- CCC-owned IP licensing
- federal / prime-contractor subcontracting
- Lock & Key / CCC-aligned restricted grant funding
- approved Culture Shock retail product lanes
- verified internal efficiency / cost avoidance

Restricted grants are not ordinary unrestricted revenue. Internal cost avoidance is economic value, not cash revenue. Employment income, contract revenue, customer revenue, restricted grant funding, and asset income remain distinct classifications.

## Stream Stage Contract

Every stream must use one of these states:

SIGNAL -> QUALIFIED -> BUILD -> FOUNDRY -> SALE_READY -> PIPELINE -> CONTRACTED -> INVOICED -> COLLECTED -> RECONCILED -> SURPLUS_ELIGIBLE -> REINVESTED

Side state: HOLD.

No dashboard may collapse those stages into a single word such as `revenue`.

## Realized Revenue Gate

A forecast is not revenue.
A signed contract is not collected revenue.
An invoice is not collected revenue.
A grant application is not an award.
An award with restrictions is not unrestricted operating revenue.
A demo metric is not financial truth.

Only COLLECTED + RECONCILED commercial receipts may enter realized-revenue totals. Revenue Assurance must preserve source, time, owner, change, validation, and provenance.

## Diversification Gate

CCC should prefer multiple independent lawful revenue wells and recurring customer relationships over dependence on one employer, customer, grant, product, platform, or speculative asset. The dashboard must eventually expose revenue by stream, contribution margin by stream, recurring versus one-time revenue, top-customer concentration, top-stream concentration, restricted versus unrestricted funding, collection risk, renewal risk, and pipeline composition.

The system does not fabricate an arbitrary concentration threshold. Management sets concentration limits from actual cash flow, risk tolerance, contract durability, liquidity, customer quality, and operating capacity.

## Freedom Architect Capital Valve

Only deployable surplus may be recommended for reinvestment. Before surplus is classified, reconcile:

1. direct delivery costs
2. accounts payable and committed costs
3. taxes and statutory obligations
4. operating reserve
5. contractual or grant restrictions
6. Family Progress Floor

No autonomous money movement is permitted. Allocation recommendations require human approval.

Every proposed reinvestment must state:

- destination
- owner
- evidence basis
- expected productive output
- downside
- liquidity effect
- dependency
- kill threshold
- review point

The preferred destination is the highest evidence-backed productive bottleneck, except that critical Trust, Ledger, SRE, SOC, legal, compliance, customer, or continuity failures override commercial optimization.

## Five-Role Economic Contract

### CCC-01 Executive Control
Reconciles all streams, identifies Pressure Loss, prevents resource drift, and decides which branch deserves the next allocation recommendation.

### CCC-02 Security & Reliability
Protects revenue and continuity, prevents avoidable losses, and converts authorized SOC/security work into reusable evidence, services, modules, compliance assets, or portfolio proof.

### CCC-03 Capital & Revenue
Owns the revenue-stream registry, contract-to-cash truth, Revenue Assurance, unit economics, grants classification, customer/commercial signals, retention economics, and capital-recycling recommendations.

### CCC-04 Career & Market Intelligence
Feeds near-term employment, contract, subcontract, and market-demand signals into the pipeline, then converts employer/customer requirements into portfolio and capability targets.

### CCC-05 Knowledge & Governance
Converts knowledge into governed reusable IP, curriculum, SOPs, data assets, licensing candidates, and protected commercial documentation while preserving lineage and legal/IP controls.

Each role reports twice per shift: where it started, what it changed, where it ended, and exactly where the next shift continues.

## Dashboard Contract

`GET /api/economics/revenue-flywheel` is a read-only endpoint. It exposes the registry, policy, stage and class counts, recurring-customer loop, customer-success gate, diversification gate, and evidence rules without inventing revenue totals. The UI may visualize the flow, but realized revenue must remain unknown until backed by collected and reconciled evidence.

## Safety and Authority

- GitHub is code authority.
- Ledger is factual/evidence authority.
- Revenue Assurance is financial truth and reconciliation authority.
- Freedom Architect is capital-allocation command.
- Living Dashboard is a read-only digital twin plus controlled request surface.
- Human approval remains mandatory for consequential financial, contractual, release, and critical infrastructure actions.

The practical meaning of an "infinite avenue" is a renewable opportunity system: continually discover, qualify, test, deliver, retain, monetize, learn, reinvest, diversify, and seed the next cycle. It is not a promise that any individual stream or the aggregate system produces infinite returns.

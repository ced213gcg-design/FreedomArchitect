## Present flaw register

### F-01 Claim-to-evidence linkage
Resume/application claims are not yet machine-linked to exact lab artifacts.

**Mitigation:** create claim IDs and evidence references before external release.

### F-02 Single application truth system
Opportunity state can fragment across job boards, chat, notes, and Drive.

**Mitigation:** one append-only pipeline ledger with duplicate suppression.

### F-03 Freshness drift
A role can close or change between discovery and submission.

**Mitigation:** same-day role freshness/remote-status verification before release.

### F-04 Public/private evidence boundary
Lab evidence can contain topology, private paths, serials, tokens, or operational details.

**Mitigation:** sanitized public portfolio views; private evidence remains internal.

### F-05 Lab-to-competency capture
Operational work is not yet guaranteed to update skill/mentor state automatically.

**Mitigation:** every lab run emits competency and mentor-grade records.

### F-06 Hardware state ambiguity
Photographed/owned hardware is not operational capability until inventoried and tested.

**Mitigation:** `PHOTO_SEEN != DEVICE_OPERATIONAL`; maintain hardware evidence states.

### F-07 Automation authority creep
Preparation automation can accidentally become unauthorized external representation.

**Mitigation:** release-policy engine; submit/contact/negotiate/accept remain human-gated until explicitly ratified.

### F-08 Revenue illusion
Offers, salary listings, interviews, or invoices can be mistaken for cash.

**Mitigation:** preserve Revenue Truth Ladder; only `COLLECTED_INCOME` is settled cash.

### F-09 Role overreach
Breadth of lab work can tempt unsupported seniority claims.

**Mitigation:** seniority is evidence-earned through repeated independent execution, diagnosis, recovery, design, and teach-back.

### F-10 Tool/vendor dependency
A single SIEM, job board, SaaS, or AI provider can become a bottleneck.

**Mitigation:** adapter architecture, local canonical state, exportable artifacts, provider independence.

### F-11 Cost-loop risk
Autonomous search/AI/report generation can consume time/API spend without improving conversion.

**Mitigation:** V-based budget guard: every recurring process must prove conversion, learning, or risk-reduction value.

### F-12 Opportunity concentration
Relying on one employment role or one employer creates economic fragility.

**Mitigation:** synchronized lanes: SOC, vulnerability, IAM/security operations, contract support, and later CCC products/services.

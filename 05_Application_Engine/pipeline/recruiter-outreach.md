# CCC Recruiter Verification & Outreach Ledger

## Verification Law
Every recruiter-supplied opportunity must separate `RECRUITER_CLAIM` from `PRIMARY_SOURCE_FACT` before application truth is established.

Verify:
- recruiter identity and company affiliation
- recruiter email/domain where applicable
- employer
- posting number
- employer career page
- compensation
- location / work setting
- required experience
- application destination

Never provide SSN, bank information, full date of birth, payment, credentials, account passwords, or authentication codes during initial recruiter contact.

## Outreach Rule
Routine drafts may be prepared internally. External recruiter messages are recorded only after an actual send event. Recruiter claims never overwrite primary employer facts.

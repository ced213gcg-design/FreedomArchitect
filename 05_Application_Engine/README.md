## Purpose
Convert verified capability, education, authorized lab performance, GitHub evidence, and technical operating discipline into truthful, versioned employment applications and recruiter conversion.

## Verification Law
A job-board or recruiter claim is a lead until reconciled to a primary employer source. Record recruiter claims separately from primary-source facts.

No generic mass resume. Every qualifying role receives the correct master variant and a versioned job-specific derivative.

## Truth Law
Missing proof does not become a claim. Coursework is not employment. Lab work is not production SOC employment. Developmental cloud/GRC/NIST terms remain explicitly qualified until evidence promotes them.

## External Action Gates
Internal discovery, verification, scoring, tailoring, evidence mapping, tracking, and reinjection may proceed autonomously within scope.

Hold for Human Command when new personal facts, legal attestations, salary commitments, background/clearance disclosures, CAPTCHA/MFA/ID verification, human assessments, interviews, offers, negotiations, or contract acceptance are required.

## Notification Law
Do not escalate routine job discoveries, rejections, keyword changes, or internal score changes. Escalate interviews, offers, recruiter requests requiring a human response, material blockers, identity/credential questions, salary negotiation, and human-only assessments.

## Reinjection Law
`JOB REQUIREMENT -> GAP -> LAB -> PERFORMANCE -> EVIDENCE -> RESUME -> NEXT APPLICATION`

No application is considered complete without versioned evidence and a submission reference.

## Governing synthesis
The financial architecture must separate at least these legal/economic objects:
1. corporate equity and ownership;
2. customer payment obligations;
3. stored-value/service credits;
4. digital tools / AI-access tokens;
5. payment stablecoins;
6. digital securities / tokenized securities;
7. Bitcoin and other digital-asset treasury holdings;
8. customer assets held in custody;
9. collateral / secured interests;
10. revenue, receivables, cash, reserves, and realized capital.

No category inherits another category's legal treatment merely because all can be represented digitally.
